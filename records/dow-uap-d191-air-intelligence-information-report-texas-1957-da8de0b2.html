DOW-UAP-D191, Air Intelligence Information Report, Texas, 1957

Department of War · 11/2/57 · Texas · Document

DOW-UAP-D191, Air Intelligence Information Report, Texas, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 11/2/57 and location Texas. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR records six observer accounts of incidents reportedly involving unidentified phenomena on November 2 and 3, 1957, in and around the municipalities of Levelland, Smyer, Shallowater, and Sundown, Texas. The report contains descriptions of bright, oval-shaped lights and objects; as well as temporary disruptions to vehicle engines, headlights, radios, and electrical systems. The file summarizes observer interviews and includes sketched depictions of reported objects. It also includes supplementary investigative materials such as a map of the area, weather data, and deconfliction with known aircraft traffic, weather-balloon activity, oil-field gas flaring, and downed power lines. The file also includes comments by the preparing officer, assessing the credibility of the individuals reports. He noted inconsistencies among the reports and noted that meteorological explanations may account for a portion of the reported activity. Ultimately, the officer stated that available knowledge within his office and the available evidence were insufficient to resolve the incident. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
