Department of War · 11/6/57 · Virginia · Document

DOW-UAP-D204, Air Intelligence Information Report, Virginia, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 11/6/57 and location Virginia. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents a November 6, 1957, incident in which three civilian observers reported an unidentified flying object (UFO) near Abingdon and Lebanon, Virginia. At approximately 1735 (5:35 p.m.) The observers described a bright red-orange object high in the sky, observed for approximately five minutes and traveling generally northwest or north. Descriptions of its apparent shape varied, including carrot-shaped, elongated egg-shaped, and elongated diamond-shaped. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
