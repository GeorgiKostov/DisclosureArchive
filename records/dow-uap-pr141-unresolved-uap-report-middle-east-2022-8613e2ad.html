DOW-UAP-PR141, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, 2022

Department of War · 2022 · Middle East · Video

DOW-UAP-PR141, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, 2022 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 2022 and location Middle East. The United States Central Command submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of 2 minutes and 36 seconds of video footage from an electro-optical (right) and infrared (left) sensor aboard a U.S. military platform in 2022. An accompanying mission report, DOW-UAP-D109, described the UAP as traveling at various speeds between 80-180 miles per hour.
