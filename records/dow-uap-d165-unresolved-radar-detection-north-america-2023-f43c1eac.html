DOW-UAP-D165, Unresolved Radar Detection, North America, 2023

Department of War · 7/15/05 · Western United States · Document

DOW-UAP-D165, Unresolved Radar Detection, North America, 2023 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 7/15/05 and location Western United States. This slide summarizes the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office s (AARO) preliminary analysis of an unresolved radar event in the western United States. In 2023, a civil air-surveillance radar system recorded eight detections over 84 seconds at an estimated altitude of 55,000 70,000 feet. If the detections were associated with a single continuous track, their cumulative point-to-point displacement would be approximately 38.5 miles, yielding a minimum mean horizontal ground-speed estimate of 1,650 mph. Successive detection segments also showed an apparent 39-degree change in bearing after approximately 48 seconds, although the available data are not sufficient to enable a comprehensive kinematic characterization. Available data do not independently establish that all detections originated from the same object or represented a continuous track. The nature and origin of the detections remain unresolved due to data insufficiency.
