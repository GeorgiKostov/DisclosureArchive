DOW-UAP-PR21, Unresolved UAP Report, Iraq, May 2022

Department of War · Iraq · Video

DOW-UAP-PR21, Unresolved UAP Report, Iraq, May 2022 is indexed as a Department of War record and location Iraq. The United States Central Command submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon (UAP) to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of ten seconds of video footage from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform in 2022. An accompanying mission report, DoW-UAP-D14, described the UAP as a probable SU-27/35."
