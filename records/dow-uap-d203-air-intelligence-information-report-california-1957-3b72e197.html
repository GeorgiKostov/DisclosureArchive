DOW-UAP-D203, Air Intelligence Information Report, California, 1957

Department of War · 9/5/57 · Canada · Document

DOW-UAP-D203, Air Intelligence Information Report, California, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 9/5/57 and location Canada. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents two observations of unidentified flying objects (UFOs) near Upland, California, on or about September 5 and 6, 1957, between 1215Z and 1230Z (5:15 a.m. and 5:30 a.m. local time). A civilian reported observing one UFO each morning, describing each as a green flash with red tails, at an estimated altitude of 2,000 feet. The reporting officer assessed that the information available was insufficient for further analysis. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
