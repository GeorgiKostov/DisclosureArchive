DOW-UAP-D209, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1955

Department of War · 3/15/55 · California · Document

DOW-UAP-D209, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1955 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 3/15/55 and location California. This file contains a U.S. Air Force Project 10073 Record Card and associated message traffic concerning a reported observation of an unidentified flying object near Avalon, Catalina Island, California, on 15 March 1955. A civilian observer described a single, very bright, round object, approximately the size of a nickel held at arm s length, that changed from orange to red and moved straight downward. The report states that the observation lasted approximately two seconds; no photographs or physical evidence accompanied the report. The associated message records clear, dry, and cool weather conditions with unrestricted visibility. An Air Force intelligence officer assessed that the object s reported flight path and brief duration in view suggested it was probably a meteor. The attached record card correspondingly records the Air Force evaluation as possibly astronomical.
