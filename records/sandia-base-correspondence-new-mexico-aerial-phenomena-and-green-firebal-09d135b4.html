Sandia Base Correspondence - New Mexico Aerial Phenomena and Green Fireballs (1948-1949)

DOW · 1948-1950 · Albuquerque, NM · Document

Sandia Base Correspondence - New Mexico Aerial Phenomena and Green Fireballs (1948-1949) is indexed as a DOW record with incident date 1948-1950 and location Albuquerque, NM. A 116-page compilation of late-1940s correspondence centered on Sandia Base, Albuquerque, New Mexico, adjacent to the atomic-weapons complex. Alongside security-inspection letters it includes the 17th District OSI 'Summary of Observations of Aerial Phenomena in the New Mexico area' the green-fireball investigation: a record of unexplained aerial-phenomena sightings concentrated in New Mexico from late 1948, recurring green fireballs near Los Alamos and Sandia, a noted period when 'flying saucer incidents in this region attained an all-time high,' unidentified lights observed over the 2nd Armored Division with positions triangulated by multiple observers, and a referenced 'Photo of Sighting No. 175.'
