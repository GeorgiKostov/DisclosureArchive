DOW-UAP-D208, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1965

Department of War · 8/8/65 · California · Document

DOW-UAP-D208, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1965 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 8/8/65 and location California. This file contains a Flying Objects Report prepared in accordance with Air Force Regulation 200-2, which established guidance for reporting unidentified flying objects, concerning an August 8, 1965, incident involving an observation of an unidentified flying object (UFO) near Palos Verdes, California. The initial report states that two civilian report observers reported a round object with flashing red, white, and blue lights at approximately 9:30 p.m. local time. They reported that the object moved between the Palos Verdes Peninsula and Catalina Island at a high estimated speed and remained in view for approximately 10 minutes. The file includes an Air Force assessment that the available information was insufficient for evaluation.
