FBI-UAP-D028, FD-302, "Dark Triangle with Lights," 2011

FBI · 2011 · Document

FBI-UAP-D028, FD-302, "Dark Triangle with Lights," 2011 is indexed as a FBI record with incident date 2011. This document is an FBI FD-302, a form the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) uses to record interviews. This FD-302 records an April 2025 interview with two former U.S. military service members, in which the interviewees describe an incident potentially involving unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) in June or July of 2011. An FBI digital rendering based upon the observers descriptions accompanies this document, under the title FBI-UAP-D029. Redactions have been made to protect the identity of eyewitnesses, the location of government facilities, or potentially sensitive information about military sites not related to UAP. No redactions have been made to any files released under President Trump's directive concerning information about the nature or existence of any encounter reported as a UAP or related phenomena.
