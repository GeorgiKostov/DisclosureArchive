DOW-UAP-D182, AAWSAP Statement of Work, August 2008

Department of War · 8/22/08 · Washington, D.C. · Document

DOW-UAP-D182, AAWSAP Statement of Work, August 2008 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 8/22/08 and location Washington, D.C.. This document is an administrative or programmatic record related to the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)-administered program active from 2008 to 2012. AAWSAP s official scope of work identified 12 technical research areas relating to potential aerospace threats over a time horizon of more than 40 years. As an administrative record, the file documents how AAWSAP was scoped, organized, tasked, funded, or described at a particular point in time. This statement of work establishes the program s formal mission for exploring far-term foreign aerospace threats by examining possible breakthrough technologies out to 2050 rather than extrapolating from then-current aerospace trends. The document lays out AAWSAP s research areas and contractual deliverables at program start, including 12 technical topics to be studied, recurring status and management reporting, comprehensive technical reports and briefings for each area, and a final integrated threat assessment. It also documents the acquisition structure of the effort, including its firm-fixed-price contracting model, small-business set-aside status, security and clearance requirements, and anticipated multi-year period of performance.
