DOW-UAP-D210, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1957

Department of War · 12/1/57 · California · Document

DOW-UAP-D210, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 12/1/57 and location California. This file contains a U.S. Air Force Project 10073 Record Card and associated material documenting six visual reports of unidentified flying objects from locations across Southern California. The reports occurred between 0306Z and 0310Z on 1 December 1957 (7:06 p.m. to 7:10 p.m. Pacific Standard Time on 30 November local time) and included locations near San Clemente Island, Borrego Springs, Blythe, Oxnard, Ventura, and San Bernardino. Civilian and military observers described a bright object moving generally northeastward. Accounts variously characterized the object as a falling star, bright light, or ball, with some reporting a bluish-white or reddish color, flame, or a trailing tail. Individual observation durations ranged from several seconds to approximately 30 seconds. The record card states that the Air Force evaluated the object to be astronomical in nature, attributing the incident to a meteor. Associated messages state that Air Defense Command personnel and Mount Wilson Observatory also assessed reported objects as meteors.
