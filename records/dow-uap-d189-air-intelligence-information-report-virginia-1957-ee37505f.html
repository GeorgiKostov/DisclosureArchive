DOW-UAP-D189, Air Intelligence Information Report, Virginia, 1957

Department of War · 10/13/57 · Virginia · Document

DOW-UAP-D189, Air Intelligence Information Report, Virginia, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 10/13/57 and location Virginia. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR records an October 13, 1957, incident approximately one half-mile south of Great Falls, Viginia, near the Potomac River. At approximately 2105Z (5:05 p.m. local time), two civilians reported observing an unidentified flying object (UFO) while attempting to locate Sputnik, a Soviet satellite. The report describes the object as a single, black, round dot with an approximate apparent size comparable to the head of a pin. The report characterizes the object as moving along a true course of 007 degrees directly overhead, as measured with a compass. It also indicates that the object remained in view for approximately 5-6 seconds before being obscured by trees. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
