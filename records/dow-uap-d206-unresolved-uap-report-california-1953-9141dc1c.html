DOW-UAP-D206, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1953

Department of War · 12/16/53 · California · Document

DOW-UAP-D206, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1953 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 12/16/53 and location California. This file contains a confirmed, complete, high-quality digitization of reports by Lockheed Aircraft Corporation personnel concerning the observation of an unidentified flying object (UFO) near Agoura and Point Mugu, California, on December 16, 1953. Clarence L. Johnson, Lockheed s Chief Engineer, transmitted the reports to the Air Technical Intelligence Center at Wright Patterson Air Force Base for scientific evaluation, along with a request for comments regarding the incident. The file also includes individual accounts from Johnson and Lockheed flight-test personnel. This report is significant, due to Johnson s reputation as one of the foremost aerospace engineers in American history, and his virtually unmatched familiarity with high-performance aircraft. Johnson reported observing a dark, elliptical object from his ranch west of Agoura at approximately 5:00 p.m. He initially considered whether it might be a cloud or smoke trail; he reported that it appeared stationary for several minutes before moving westward and disappearing. Separately, personnel aboard a Lockheed test flight reported seeing an object in the vicinity of Point Mugu while flying off the southern California coast at approximately the same time. Their accounts described an apparently stationary, dark, wing-like or elongated object that moved westward and disappeared from view.
