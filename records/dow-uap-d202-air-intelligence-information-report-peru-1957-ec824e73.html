DOW-UAP-D202, Air Intelligence Information Report, Peru, 1957

Department of War · 11/8/57 · Peru · Document

DOW-UAP-D202, Air Intelligence Information Report, Peru, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 11/8/57 and location Peru. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents reported observations of an unidentified flying object (UFO) near Cajamarca, Peru. A weather observer at the Compa a Peruana de Servicios Meteorol gicos observatory, approximately 35 miles south of Cajamarca, reported sightings on October 26, 28, and 29 and November 8, 1957. The observer described a white-steel-gleaming object. The report contains descriptive observations and measurement data but did not identify the object or provide a final analytical conclusion on its nature or origin. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
