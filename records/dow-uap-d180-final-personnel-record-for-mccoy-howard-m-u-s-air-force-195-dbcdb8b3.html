DOW-UAP-D180, Final Personnel Record for McCoy, Howard M., U.S. Air Force, 1954

Department of War · 8/31/54 · Document

DOW-UAP-D180, Final Personnel Record for McCoy, Howard M., U.S. Air Force, 1954 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 8/31/54. This file contains the final personnel record of U. S. Air Force officer Howard M. Mc Coy, who left service as a Colonel, a senior field-grade officer responsible for significant operational and administrative duties. From December 1946 to August 1949, Colonel Mc Coy served as Deputy Commanding General for Intelligence (T-2), and later as Chief of the Intelligence Department, Headquarters, Air Materiel Command, at what is now known as Wright-Patterson Air Force Base. During his tenure, the Air Force initiated two significant studies centering on unidentified anomalous phenomena: Project SIGN and Project GRUDGE. He was among the senior Air Force intelligence officers involved in the early design, administration, and execution of those programs.
