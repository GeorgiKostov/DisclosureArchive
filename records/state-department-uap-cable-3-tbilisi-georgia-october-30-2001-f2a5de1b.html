State Department UAP Cable 3, Tbilisi, Georgia, October 30, 2001

Department of State · 10/28/2001-10/29/2001 · Georgia · Document

State Department UAP Cable 3, Tbilisi, Georgia, October 30, 2001 is indexed as a Department of State record with incident date 10/28/2001-10/29/2001 and location Georgia. On October 28-29, there was an incident alleged by the Georgian Foreign Ministry that Russian aircraft had violated Georgian airspace and bombed areas of the Kodori Gorge. Russians denied any of the claims and said that it could have been UFOs. Cable authors note that Russians typically engage in the bold lie when they wish to conceal actions.
