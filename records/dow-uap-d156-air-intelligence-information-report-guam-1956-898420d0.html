DOW-UAP-D156, Air Intelligence Information Report, Guam, 1956

Department of War · 12/31/56 · Guam · Document

DOW-UAP-D156, Air Intelligence Information Report, Guam, 1956 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 12/31/56 and location Guam. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U. S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U. S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR records a December 31, 1956, incident in the vicinity of Guam in which the pilot of a U. S. Air Force F-86D aircraft reported observing an unidentified aerial blinking light. The report notes that a lighted weather balloon had been launched before the sighting and states that, if the balloon had malfunctioned and remained at a relatively low altitude, it could possibly explain the reported object. It also indicates that the preparing officer did not regard the balloon explanation as sufficient unless that condition applied. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
