CIA-UAP-D026, Summary Assessment of "Flying Saucers," 1952

CIA · 8/1/52 · Washington, D.C. · Document

CIA-UAP-D026, Summary Assessment of "Flying Saucers," 1952 is indexed as a CIA record with incident date 8/1/52 and location Washington, D.C.. This 1 August 1952 memorandum from Edward Tauss, Acting Chief of the Weapons and Equipment Division in the Central Intelligence Agency s (CIA) Office of Scientific Intelligence, provides an initial assessment of flying saucers and associated reports. The memorandum states that the Air Technical Intelligence Center had received between 1,000 and 2,000 reports, many of which it assessed as false, attributable to known U.S. equipment, or explainable as natural phenomena. It states that fewer than 100 reasonably credible reports remained unexplained and that the available information did not identify a consistent pattern in their reported characteristics or locations. The memorandum recommends that CIA continue to monitor the issue in coordination with the Air Technical Intelligence Center while avoiding public disclosure of CIA interest. It also notes that Tauss had arranged a comprehensive briefing with the Air Technical Intelligence Center at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base for 8 August 1952 and would prepare a detailed analysis afterward.
