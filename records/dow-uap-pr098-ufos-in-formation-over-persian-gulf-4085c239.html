Department of War · CENTCOM · Video

DOW-UAP-PR098, "UFOs in formation over Persian Gulf?" is indexed as a Department of War record and location CENTCOM. On March 6, 2026, eight members of the U.S. House of Representatives requested access to 51 potentially UAP-related records allegedly held by the Department of War and the Intelligence Community. The All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) identified a collection of responsive materials held on a classified network. Many of these materials lack a substantiated chain-of-custody.
