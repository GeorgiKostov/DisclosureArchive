DOW-UAP-D199, Air Intelligence Information Report, Iran, 1957

Department of War · 10/23/57 · Iran · Document

DOW-UAP-D199, Air Intelligence Information Report, Iran, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 10/23/57 and location Iran. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents an October 23, 1957, incident in which a U.S. Army observer reported a high-altitude vapor trail proceeding from east to west over Tehran, Iran, for about three and a half minutes. The reporter states that he did not directly observe any object associated with the vapor trail, and that he did not perceive any sound such an object may have produced. The report continues by describing the trail as entering or passing above thin cirrus clouds to the west and disappearing. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
