DOW-UAP-D195, Air Intelligence Information Report, New Mexico, 1957

Department of War · 11/4/57 · New Mexico · Document

DOW-UAP-D195, Air Intelligence Information Report, New Mexico, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 11/4/57 and location New Mexico. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents a November 4, 1957, incident in the vicinity of Kirtland Air Force Base, New Mexico, at approximately 10:45 p.m. Mountain Standard Time. Two U.S. Air Force servicemembers positioned in the Kirtland control tower reported observing a white light and an object through binoculars; the observation lasted approximately five to six minutes. A Kirtland radar operator also reported an unidentified radar return that appeared similar to a conventional aircraft return. A subsequent Air Force investigation reviewed radar information, local weather conditions, and possible astronomical phenomena; the latter check found no unusual or distinctive astronomical event that night. The preparing officer assessed that the object may have been an unidentified standard aircraft operating in the area. The report includes a diagram depicting the reported flight path near the control tower, radar site, runway, and the area identified as Drumhead. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
