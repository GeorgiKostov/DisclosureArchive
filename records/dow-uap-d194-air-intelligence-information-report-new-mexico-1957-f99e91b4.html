DOW-UAP-D194, Air Intelligence Information Report, New Mexico, 1957

Department of War · 11/4/57 · New Mexico · Document

DOW-UAP-D194, Air Intelligence Information Report, New Mexico, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 11/4/57 and location New Mexico. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents a November 4, 1957, incident involving a report of an unidentified flying object approximately 8 10 miles south of Orogrande, New Mexico, on U.S. Highway 54. The reporting individual stated that an egg-shaped object made two apparent passes near the Sacramento and San Andres Mountains. An Air Force follow-on inquiry found no unidentified aircraft reported in the area. The report also noted inconsistencies in the observer s account and noted the absence of corroborating accounts from any third-party observer. The preparing officer assessed that the observation was possibly an optical phenomenon, noting that mirages were not unusual over the area s flat, light-colored terrain. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
