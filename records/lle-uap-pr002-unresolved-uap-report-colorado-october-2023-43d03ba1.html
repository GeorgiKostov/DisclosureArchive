LLE-UAP-PR002, Unresolved UAP Report, Colorado, October 2023

Local Law Enforcement · October, 2023 · Colorado · Video

LLE-UAP-PR002, Unresolved UAP Report, Colorado, October 2023 is indexed as a Local Law Enforcement record with incident date October, 2023 and location Colorado. A local law enforcement officer submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office consisting of 15 minutes of video footage from a commercially available cellular device s rear-facing camera in October 2023. The reporting officer described the phenomenon as silent and as featuring red, blue, and green flashing lights. AARO Comment: Cellular devices of the type used to capture this footage employ autofocus and other automated image-processing functions that may distort the apparent morphological and kinematic characteristics of distant subjects, particularly at high magnification.
