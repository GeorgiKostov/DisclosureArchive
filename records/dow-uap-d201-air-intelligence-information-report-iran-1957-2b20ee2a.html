DOW-UAP-D201, Air Intelligence Information Report, Iran, 1957

Department of War · 5/10/05 · Iran · Document

DOW-UAP-D201, Air Intelligence Information Report, Iran, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 5/10/05 and location Iran. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents several sightings of unidentified flying objects (UFOs) over Iranian Azerbaijan between May and October 1957. It cites reporting by a local media outlet, Ettilaat, of a comet over Rezaieh on May 21, and later coverage of an incident in October that the outlet speculated may have been a sighting of Sputnik I, a Soviet satellite. The report also refers to July sightings by Americans in Tabriz of a large red light moving east to west after sunset and an October 14 sighting south of Tabriz, at approximately 2:30 p.m, of a bright white object moving east to west. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
