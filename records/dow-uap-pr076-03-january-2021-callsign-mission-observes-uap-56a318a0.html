DOW-UAP-PR076, "03 January 2021 [CALLSIGN] (Mission) observes UAP" is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 2021 and location CENTCOM. On March 6, 2026, eight members of the U.S. House of Representatives requested access to 51 potentially UAP-related records allegedly held by the Department of War and the Intelligence Community.
