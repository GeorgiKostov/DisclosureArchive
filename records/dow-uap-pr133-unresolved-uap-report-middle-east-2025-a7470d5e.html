DOW-UAP-PR133, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, 2025

Department of War · 2025 · Middle East · Video

DOW-UAP-PR133, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, 2025 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 2025 and location Middle East. The United States Central Command submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of 4 minutes and 58 seconds of video footage from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform in 2024. An accompanying mission report, DOW-UAP-D107, described the UAP as look[ing] like a white round orbit moving as fast as 500 miles per hour.
