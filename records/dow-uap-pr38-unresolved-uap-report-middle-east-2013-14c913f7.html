DOW-UAP-PR38, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, 2013

Department of War · Middle East · Video

DOW-UAP-PR38, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, 2013 is indexed as a Department of War record and location Middle East. The United States Central Command submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of one minute and 46 seconds of video footage from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform in 2013. The reporter did not provide any oral or written description of the observation.
