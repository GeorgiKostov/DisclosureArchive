ODNI USPER Narrative - Senior Intelligence Officer Orb Encounter (2025)

ODNI · Late 2025 · Western United States · Document

ODNI USPER Narrative - Senior Intelligence Officer Orb Encounter (2025) is indexed as a ODNI record with incident date Late 2025 and location Western United States. A first-person narrative by a senior U.S. intelligence officer describing a late-2025 helicopter mission over a test range to investigate loud thuds and several nights of UAP sightings. The officer describes over an hour of close encounters: a 'super-hot' object on FLIR that split in two, an object that rose to within ten feet of the helicopter, two large oval orange orbs with white or yellow centers flaring just above the rotor disk, a four-to-five orb 'T' formation that dimmed in reverse order, and orange orbs that appeared above fighter jets at roughly 23,000 feet and seemed to 'chase' them. The officer states he did not photograph the event.

Notable / anomalous element

  • Our mission was to investigate loud thuds heard in the mountains on the test range, which coincided with Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) sightings reported over the previous several nights. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • We aimed to search remote mountain areas for possible debris or objects that might explain the orb-like sightings. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • The object then split into two and changed direction. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • In the distance, we saw countless orange orbs swarming in all directions against the backdrop of the mountain. (native PDF text, page 2)

Details

  • In late 2025, during early evening daylight hours, I a senior U.S. intelligence officer along with a colleague and two pilots, departed our Joint Operations Center (JOC) in a helicopter. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • Our mission was to investigate loud thuds heard in the mountains on the test range, which coincided with Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) sightings reported over the previous several nights. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • We aimed to search remote mountain areas for possible debris or objects that might explain the orb-like sightings. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • After leaving the JOC, we flew a low-altitude map of the earth route through the mountain range for several hours. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • Multiple times, we spotted debris on the ground and descended for closer inspection. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • We noted the location and then pressed on. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • Running low on fuel, we headed to a prearranged rendezvous point to meet a ground team and let my colleague disembark. (native PDF text, page 1)
  • The two pilots and I then proceeded to a prepositioned tanker on the range for refueling. (native PDF text, page 1)
