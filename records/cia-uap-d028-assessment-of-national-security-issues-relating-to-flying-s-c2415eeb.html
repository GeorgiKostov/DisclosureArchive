CIA-UAP-D028, Assessment of National Security Issues Relating to "Flying Saucers," 1952

CIA · 10/13/52 · Washington, D.C. · Document

CIA-UAP-D028, Assessment of National Security Issues Relating to "Flying Saucers," 1952 is indexed as a CIA record with incident date 10/13/52 and location Washington, D.C.. This 13 October 1952 memorandum from S. Q. Reber, Assistant Director for Intelligence Coordination, to the Deputy Director (Intelligence) assesses flying saucers as a potential national security concern. It notes that the U.S. Air Force had investigated approximately 1,500 reported sightings through the Air Technical Intelligence Center and had explained all but approximately 20 percent of them. The memorandum states that the Office of Scientific Intelligence had not concluded that the reported objects were created or controlled by the Soviet Union. It identifies priority intelligence questions concerning Soviet knowledge of the phenomena, potential Soviet ability to exploit them against U.S. security interests, and possible effects on the U.S. warning system. It also concludes that the Department of Defense held primary responsibility for initiating fundamental scientific research to develop a comprehensive understanding of the phenomena and improve rapid identification. The memorandum recommends that the Director of Central Intelligence communicate with Secretary of Defense Robert A. Lovett to formalize areas of responsibility as they pertain to unidentified flying objects. It further concludes that available information did not yet provide a basis for psychological-warfare planners to consider employing the phenomena and that a National Intelligence Estimate should precede any development of a public policy intended to reduce or restrain public concern.
