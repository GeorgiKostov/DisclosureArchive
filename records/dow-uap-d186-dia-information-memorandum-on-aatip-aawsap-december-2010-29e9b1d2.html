DOW-UAP-D186, DIA Information Memorandum on AATIP (AAWSAP), December 2010 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 12/10/26 and location Washington, D.C.. This document is an administrative or programmatic record related to the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)-administered program active from 2008 to 2012. AAWSAP s official scope of work identified 12 technical research areas relating to potential aerospace threats over a time horizon of more than 40 years. As an administrative record, the file documents how AAWSAP was scoped, organized, tasked, funded, or described at a particular point in time. This December 2010 DIA information memorandum provides a retrospective summary of the Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP), describing its congressional origins, contract execution, and accumulated outputs through the end of Fiscal Year 2010. The memo states that the effort was first funded in the Fiscal Year 2008 Defense Supplemental Appropriation Act through a $10 million appropriation sponsored by Senators Harry Reid (D-NV) and Daniel Inouye (D-HI), and that DIA subsequently awarded the contract to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies LLC (BAASS), the only bidder. It reports that BAASS produced 26 technical reports in the initial phase and that a further $12 million appropriation in Fiscal Year 2010 supported production of 12 additional reports. The memo also recounts Senator Reid s June 2009 request for special access protections for the program and DIA s subsequent conclusion that there were insufficient grounds to classify the program at that level. It concludes by recounting an earlier determination that the program offered limited value to DIA, but upon completion of the term of the contract, could be transitioned to another agency better suited to oversee it.
