DOW-UAP-D183-Correspondence from Senator Harry Reid, June 2009 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 6/24/09 and location Washington, D.C.. This document is an administrative or programmatic record related to the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)-administered program active from 2008 to 2012. AAWSAP s official scope of work identified 12 technical research areas relating to potential aerospace threats over a time horizon of more than 40 years. As an administrative record, the file documents how AAWSAP was scoped, organized, tasked, funded, or described at a particular point in time. This June 2009 letter from Senator Harry Reid (D-NV) to Deputy Secretary of Defense William Lynn III requests that portions of the Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) be placed under a restricted Special Access Program (SAP) with a tightly controlled access list. The letter states that the Senate had directed DIA to assess far-term foreign advanced aerospace threats, describes ongoing technical work aligning with AAWSAP s program areas, and asserts that the program had already produced several highly sensitive unconventional findings. On that basis, Reid states that ordinary classified information handling practices, and even conventional SAP procedures, would be insufficient to safeguard some parts of the effort, particularly those involving collection methodology, engineering application, industry participation, and the identities of specialized personnel. The attached material proposes a preliminary SAP structure, including a bigoted-access list, a tentative program timeline through Fiscal Year 2013, and continued roles for both government and BAASS personnel. AARO Comment: In the administrative record surrounding this program, Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) appears in some documents as an alternate name for the same underlying AAWSAP effort, rather than as a wholly separate program. In this context, Senator Reid used the term in public correspondence in lieu of the AAWSAP program name.
