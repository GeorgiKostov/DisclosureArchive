EOP-UAP-D002, Memorandum on Interagency Coordination for the Recovery of Foreign Materiel, May 1963

Executive Office of the President · 5/29/63 · Washington, D.C. · Document

EOP-UAP-D002, Memorandum on Interagency Coordination for the Recovery of Foreign Materiel, May 1963 is indexed as a Executive Office of the President record with incident date 5/29/63 and location Washington, D.C.. This document is an internal National Aeronautics and Space Council (NASC) memorandum from staff member R. W. Hale to Executive Secretary Edward C. Welsh. NASC was a 1958 1973 body within the Executive Office of the President responsible at the time for coordinating U. S. space policy. The memorandum records a May 28, 1963, meeting with Arnold Frutkin, Director of International Programs at the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), concerning the development of a coordinated interagency policy for recovering reentering U. S. and Soviet space debris and satellite fragments that might land on foreign soil. This memorandum is associated with a larger body of interagency correspondence included in this collection under the title USG-UAP-D002. The memorandum highlights an interagency policy debate over organizational jurisdiction during the early Space Race. It states that the Department of State and Department of War sought to leverage NASA s standing as a civilian scientific agency to help facilitate discreet recovery of sensitive space hardware while publicly returning non-sensitive components to the country of origin. As described in the memorandum, the proposed arrangement would have served several purposes: enabling access to adversary technologies, protecting U. S. technologies, preserving diplomatic goodwill, and obscuring the intelligence gathering dimension of technology recovery operations. The memorandum also presents Director Frutkin s reaction to the proposed arrangement as very negative.
