FBI-UAP-D024, FD-302, "Airborne Lights and Triangle," 2002, 2023-2024

FBI · 2002, 2023-2024 · Northeastern U.S.; Afghanistan · Document

FBI-UAP-D024, FD-302, "Airborne Lights and Triangle," 2002, 2023-2024 is indexed as a FBI record with incident date 2002, 2023-2024 and location Northeastern U.S.; Afghanistan. This document is an FBI FD-302, a form the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) uses to record interviews. This FD-302 records a March 2024 interview with a former U.S. military pilot, in which the interviewee describes several incidents potentially involving unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) in 2023 and 2024. The interviewee also recalls a 2002 incident potentially involving UAP, while deployed as a military reservist in Afghanistan. An FBI digital rendering based upon the observer s description accompanies this document, under the title FBI-UAP-D025. Redactions have been made to protect the identity of eyewitnesses, the location of government facilities, or potentially sensitive information about military sites not related to UAP. No redactions have been made to any files released under President Trump's directive concerning information about the nature or existence of any encounter reported as a UAP or related phenomena.
