CIA-UAP-D027, Office of Scientific Intelligence Briefing on "Flying Saucers," 1952

CIA · 8/14/52 · Washington, D.C. · Document

CIA-UAP-D027, Office of Scientific Intelligence Briefing on "Flying Saucers," 1952 is indexed as a CIA record with incident date 8/14/52 and location Washington, D.C.. This 14 August 1952 Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) Office of Scientific Intelligence (OSI) briefing evaluates the U.S. Air Force s investigation of flying saucer reports and considers their potential intelligence implications. OSI prepared the briefing amid heightened public and official interest following an increase in reports during July 1952 and recent public statements by U.S. Air Force officials. The briefing summarizes the history of reported sightings, the Air Technical Intelligence Center s (ATIC) reporting and analytical processes, selected unexplained cases, and several theories advanced to explain the reports. The materials state that approximately 1,500 official reports had been received since 1947, including 250 during July 1952, and that ATIC categorized approximately 20 percent as unexplained. It states that OSI reviewed its own intelligence holdings, official reports, press and popular accounts, and Soviet press indexes; consulted an Air Force special-study representative and ATIC personnel at Wright Field; and sought input from selected scientific consultants. The briefing states that OSI recommended no further action following those inquiries.
