CIA-UAP-D029, Official Diary Entry on "Flying Saucers," 1952

CIA · 10/28/52 · Washington, D.C. · Document

CIA-UAP-D029, Official Diary Entry on "Flying Saucers," 1952 is indexed as a CIA record with incident date 10/28/52 and location Washington, D.C.. This 28 October 1952 entry in a Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) official diary records a discussion of new information concerning flying saucers. Mr. Clark reported that CIA had received additional information, including reports of objects observed from the ground by individuals the entry characterized as relatively reliable. The entry notes that the absence of reported noise or flaring associated with certain reports posed a theoretical difficulty for interpreting them. It further states that several scientists had presented papers describing a possible propulsion method that would not involve noise or flares. Dr. Clark proposed a briefing on the subject and recommended bringing the information to the attention of the U.S. Air Force and the Research and Development Board; the diary s author concurred.
