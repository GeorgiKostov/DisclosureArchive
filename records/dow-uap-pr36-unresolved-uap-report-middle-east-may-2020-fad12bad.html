DOW-UAP-PR36, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, May 2020

Department of War · Middle East · Video

DOW-UAP-PR36, Unresolved UAP Report, Middle East, May 2020 is indexed as a Department of War record and location Middle East. The United States Central Command submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon (UAP) to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of two minutes and 17 seconds of video footage from an infrared sensor aboard a U.S. military platform in 2020. An accompanying Range Fouler report, DoW-UAP-D38, described the UAP as a solid white object making erratic movements above the water.
