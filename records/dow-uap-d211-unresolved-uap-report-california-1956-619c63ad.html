DOW-UAP-D211, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1956

Department of War · 8/21/56 · California · Document

DOW-UAP-D211, Unresolved UAP Report, California, 1956 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 8/21/56 and location California. This file contains a U.S. Air Force Project 10073 Record Card and associated materials concerning a reported observation of an unidentified flying object near Fontana, California, on August 21, 1956. A civilian Ground Observer Corps supervisor reported seeing one round, red object with a tail. The observer described the object as approximately the size of a basketball at arm s length, moving eastward, and disappearing into fog after approximately two minutes. No photographs or physical evidence accompanied the report. The associated record card assesses the reported object as probably a jet aircraft, with the apparent tail likely a contrail, though lists the cause as undetermined at the time of the initial duty officer review.
