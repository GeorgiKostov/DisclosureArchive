DOW-UAP-D197, Air Intelligence Information Report, Michigan, 1957

Department of War · 10/13/57 · Michigan · Document

DOW-UAP-D197, Air Intelligence Information Report, Michigan, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 10/13/57 and location Michigan. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR documents an October 13, 1957, incident involving an unidentified flying object (UFO) near Indian Trails on Lake Erie, Michigan. At approximately 10:05 p.m. local time, two observers reported a single silver-gray, bell-shaped object, with an apparent size comparable to a basketball, and a visible division between its upper and lower sections, featuring possible lights or portholes, and emitting a high-pitched humming sound. The observers reported that the object initially appeared similar to a falling object, then leveled off at an estimated 1,500 feet and traveled from south-southwest to north-northeast for approximately 8 10 seconds before being obscured by trees. The report estimated the object s speed at about 225 mph and distance from the observers to be approximately 150 yards. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
