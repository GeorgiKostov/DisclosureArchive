FBI-UAP-D012, Newark Field Office, 1952-1967 is indexed as a FBI record with incident date August 1952-1967 and location New Jersey, United States. This file details a special inquiry by the Federal Bureau of Investigation s Newark Field Office of various reported sightings of unidentified flying objects (UFOs) in and near New Jersey between August 1952 and January 1967. These files include memoranda and correspondence between the FBI and the U.S. Air Force regarding UFO sightings, as well as public correspondence concerning various UFO-focused groups.
