DOW-UAP-PR22, Unresolved UAP Report, Syria, July 2022

Department of War · Syria · Video

DOW-UAP-PR22, Unresolved UAP Report, Syria, July 2022 is indexed as a Department of War record and location Syria. The United States Central Command submitted a report of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon (UAP) to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) consisting of 14 seconds of video footage from an infrared (left) and electro-optical (right) sensor aboard a U.S. military platform in 2022. An accompanying mission report, DoW-UAP-D16, described the UAP as moving from north to south. Video Description: At the five second mark, the video depicts an object moving from right to left across the top right quarter of the sensor field-of-view.
