DOW-UAP-D190, Air Intelligence Information Report, Massachusetts, 1957

Department of War · 10/12/57 · Massachusetts · Document

DOW-UAP-D190, Air Intelligence Information Report, Massachusetts, 1957 is indexed as a Department of War record with incident date 10/12/57 and location Massachusetts. This document is an Air Intelligence Information Report (AIIR), a standardized U.S. Air Force reporting form used to record raw information of potential intelligence value for later analysis. The form served as a primary vehicle for U.S. Air Force field intelligence collection throughout the early Cold War, until defense-wide reporting standards were established in the early 1960s. This AIIR records an October 12, 1957, incident 800 yards off Martha s Vineyard and one to two miles east-southeast of Woods Hole, Massachusetts. At approximately 2035Z (4:35 p.m. local time), a group of two Air Force officers, two sailing masters assigned to research vessels with the Oceangraph Institute, and a civilian reported observing a cylindrical, metallic-colored object with protrusions while fishing. The report describes the object as appearing to descend rapidly, changing direction twice, and disappearing at high speed after approximately 20 seconds. The reporting office stated that it did not conduct further investigation or analysis of the report. All descriptive and estimative language contained in this report reflects the reporter s subjective interpretation at the time of the event. Such characterizations should not be interpreted as a conclusive indication of the presence or absence of any intrinsic object features or performance characteristics.
